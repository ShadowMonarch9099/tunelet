# Tunelet promo: storyboard

1920×1080, 30 fps, ~42 s. An animated web page (`video.html`, GSAP timeline) rendered frame by frame with Edge, plus real screen recordings of Tunelet, encoded with ffmpeg.

## Cast

One person, three looks. They swap with a circular wipe in Tunelet's colours, to say "this is anyone".

| Look | Who | Details | On their screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Student | Curly hair, round glasses, purple hoodie, white over-ear headphones | Lecture notes |
| B | Developer | Teal beanie, dark tee with `</>`, black headphones | Code editor |
| C | Everyday music lover | Long locs, gold hoops, denim jacket, earbuds | Browser / work doc |

## Scenes

| Time | Scene | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Vibing** | The student works at the laptop, nodding to music; notes float up. |
| 3–7.5 s | **Anyone** | Wipe → developer, wipe → music lover. Each vibes at their own work. |
| 7.5–17 s | **The problem** | A song they want to skip comes on. Smile drops; they hit Alt+Tab; the screen flips through windows, they lose their place, frustration builds (frown, steam, screen shake). It happens to each look in turn, faster each time. |
| 17–20.5 s | **Reveal** | Cut to dark. The Pulse logo builds: ring sweeps round, bars bounce, "Tunelet" slides in. |
| 20.5–37 s | **Montage (real app)** | Real recordings of Tunelet, each with a short caption: never leaves your app · one-click menu · search & play · colours match the album · synced lyrics · one key to hide. |
| 37–42 s | **Payoff** | The character, cycling through all three looks, vibes happily with the Tunelet bubble beside their screen. End card: logo, "Free for Windows & Mac", GitHub link. |
