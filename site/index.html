Download for Windows

Your Mac

Mac beta

macOS 11 or newer

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. One app for both.

Download for Mac

 

Drag Tunelet into Applications. The first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Then connect Spotify. It takes about two minutes with the setup guide. Already installed? Tunelet updates itself.
